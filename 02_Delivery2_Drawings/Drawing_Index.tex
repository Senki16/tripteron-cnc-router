\section{About this package}

This package contains the complete set of manufacturing and assembly drawings of the
Tripteron CNC router. The drawing sheets that follow this index are the CAD sheets as
issued from SolidWorks; they are reproduced unchanged so that the dimensions, tolerances
and revision state shown on them remain the ones the parts were made to.

\textbf{General notes applying to every sheet:}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,nosep]
  \item \textbf{All dimensions are in millimetres (mm).} Angles are in degrees.
  \item Projection: first-angle (ISO-A).
  \item Sheet format: A3 unless stated otherwise on the sheet.
  \item Unless otherwise indicated: linear tolerance $\pm\SI{0.5}{\milli\metre}$,
        angular tolerance $\pm\SI{0.5}{\degree}$, break all sharp edges.
  \item Do not scale the drawing; work from the stated dimensions.
  \item Materials are stated in the title block of each sheet; the structural members are
        steel tube and plate, the links are $25\times25\times\SI{2}{\milli\metre}$ square
        steel tube.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Note on the sheets that follow.} The CAD sheets are reproduced as issued in the
original submission, so their title blocks are in Spanish and carry the revision data of
that submission. The reading key is: \emph{Materia} = course, \emph{Proyecto} = project,
\emph{Dibuj\'o} = drawn by, \emph{Verif.} = checked, \emph{Aprob.} = approved,
\emph{Escala} = scale, \emph{Unidades} = units, \emph{Cantidad} = quantity,
\emph{Formato} = sheet size, \emph{Material} = material, \emph{Hoja 1 de 1} = sheet 1 of 1,
\emph{Las cotas se expresan en mm} = all dimensions in millimetres, \emph{No cambie la
escala} = do not scale the drawing, \emph{Romper aristas} = break sharp edges.
